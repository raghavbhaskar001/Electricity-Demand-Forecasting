## 1. Foundational Load Forecasting & Deep Learning Literature

1. **Chen, K., Chen, K., Wang, Q., He, Z., Hu, J., & He, J. (2017).**  
   *Short-term load forecasting with deep residual networks / Electric load forecasting based on Long Short-Term Memory network.*  
   Foundational reference motivating the comparison of convolutional, recurrent (LSTM), and hybrid sequence architectures for short-term electrical load forecasting.

2. **Hochreiter, S., & Schmidhuber, J. (1997).**  
   *Long short-term memory.*  
   Neural Computation, 9(8), 1735–1780. `https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.1997.9.8.1735`

3. **Shi, H., Xu, M., & Li, R. (2018).**  
   *Deep learning for household load forecasting—A novel pooling deep RNN.*  
   IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, 9(5), 5271–5280. `https://doi.org/10.1109/TSG.2017.2686012`

4. **Huber, P. J. (1964).**  
   *Robust estimation of a location parameter.*  
   The Annals of Mathematical Statistics, 35(1), 73–101. `https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177703732`
